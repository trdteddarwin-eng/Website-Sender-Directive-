# Scene Design Document: NeoCell Collagen UGC Ad

**Product:** NeoCell Collagen Bio-Peptides Powder
**Avatar:** Muscular young Black man, shirtless, buzz cut, lean defined physique, wearing light blue jeans (avatar source image is a half-body kitchen shot)
**Total Duration:** 25 seconds (3 scenes: 5s + 10s + 10s)
**Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 vertical
**Date:** 2026-02-24

---

## Scene 1: THE HOOK (5 seconds)

### Creative Intent
Stop the scroll. A muscular shirtless man in a gym looking directly at camera with confrontational energy, delivering a challenge. The physique IS the hook — it demands attention on fitness TikTok.

### Environment
Modern commercial gym interior. Black rubber gym flooring with subtle texture. A chrome dumbbell rack spanning the wall behind him, loaded with dumbbells ranging from 20lb to 80lb. A flat weight bench sits to his right with a white gym towel draped over one end and a black water bottle resting on the seat. Further back, a cable machine and a squat rack are partially visible. The walls are dark charcoal gray. Overhead, two rows of recessed LED strip lights emit a warm-white glow (3500K color temperature), creating defined shadows under his pecs and abs. The overall atmosphere is a slightly dim evening gym session — not bright, not dark, just moody and real.

### Camera
Medium shot, framed from mid-chest up to just above the top of his head. Camera is at eye level, approximately 3 feet away — the exact distance of someone holding their phone to film a friend. Slight natural handheld wobble throughout (not stabilized, not shaky — just human). No zoom, no pan. Static framing with the micro-movements of a real person filming.

### Lighting
Primary: Overhead LED strips creating downward illumination that catches the contours of his shoulders, chest, and deltoids. Secondary: Ambient warm fill from the gym's general lighting hitting the dumbbell rack chrome, creating soft metallic reflections in the background. No dramatic side-lighting. The look is "gym at 7pm" — well-lit enough to see detail, dim enough to feel atmospheric. Warm color temperature throughout.

### Actor Action
He faces the camera directly. His posture is squared up, shoulders back, chest slightly forward — confident, not aggressive. His right hand comes up in a pointing gesture (finger extended toward camera) as he starts talking. His expression begins confident and knowing — a slight smirk, one eyebrow slightly raised — then shifts to incredulous ("you're really not doing this?") with a subtle head shake. His left hand rests naturally at his side. At the 4-second mark, he drops the pointing hand, his gaze shifts slightly right toward the dumbbell rack, and he begins turning his body to the right, reaching with his right hand toward a dumbbell on the rack. Last frame: side profile, arm extended toward the rack.

### Props in Frame
- Chrome dumbbell rack (background, in focus)
- Flat weight bench with white towel and black water bottle (right side of frame)
- Cable machine partially visible (deep background, slightly out of focus)
- Black rubber gym flooring

## Scene 2: THE WORKOUT + PRODUCT REVEAL (10 seconds)

### Creative Intent
Two-part scene. First half: raw workout footage — heavy curls, muscular effort, sweat. This validates his credibility. Second half: the pivot — he sets the weight down, walks to a bench where the NeoCell tub and shaker are waiting, scoops powder, shakes it up. The product integrates naturally into his routine. It's not a commercial reveal; it's just what he does between sets.

### Environment
Same gym, now shown wider. The dumbbell rack is to his left. A large wall mirror spans the back wall, reflecting parts of the gym and creating depth. Two weight machines (lat pulldown, seated row) are visible in the mid-background. The flat bench from Scene 1 is now positioned center-right of the frame, and on it sits: a blue NeoCell Collagen tub (label facing slightly toward camera), a dark translucent shaker bottle with a black lid, the same white towel, and the black water bottle. Black rubber flooring continues throughout. Same warm-white overhead LED lighting. The gym has 2-3 other pieces of equipment visible in the background — the space feels real and populated with gear, not a sparse set.

### Camera
Starts at a medium-wide angle to capture the full curl motion (head to thigh visible, plus some gym background). The framing has the same handheld wobble quality. As he finishes the last curl and walks toward the bench (around the 5-second mark), the camera follows with a subtle pan right and tightens slightly to a medium shot as he reaches the bench. By the time he's scooping powder, the framing is tighter — his hands, the blue tub, and the shaker are all clearly visible. The blue NeoCell label should be readable.

### Lighting
Same warm-white overhead LEDs from Scene 1. The wider shot reveals more of the gym's lighting: a second row of LED strips farther back, and the metallic surfaces of the weight machines catching and scattering small highlights. The wall mirror adds reflected light, slightly brightening the scene compared to Scene 1 but maintaining the same color temperature and mood. When he's curling, the overhead light catches his biceps and shoulders at peak contraction — the muscle definition is accentuated by the top-down illumination.

### Actor Action
**Seconds 0-5 (Workout):** He grips a heavy dumbbell (50-60lb range) in his right hand and performs 3-4 controlled bicep curls. His form is strict — elbow pinned to his side, full range of motion, slow negative on the way down. His face shows focused intensity: jaw slightly clenched, eyes fixed forward, a vein visible on his forearm and neck. His left hand may rest on a bench or his hip for stability. Slight sweat sheen on his forehead, shoulders, and chest. After the last rep, he exhales, lowers the dumbbell to the rack.

**Seconds 5-10 (Product):** He takes one step to the right toward the bench. He picks up the blue NeoCell tub with his left hand, holds it at mid-torso height (label visible), and uses his right hand to scoop white powder from the tub with a small scoop. He tips the scoop into the open shaker bottle, sets the tub back on the bench, snaps the shaker lid closed, and gives it 3-4 quick shakes with one hand. He brings the shaker to his lips and takes a drink. His expression shifts from workout intensity to casual satisfaction — a slight nod, relaxed brow, the ghost of a smile. This is routine for him, not a performance.

### Props in Frame

- Chrome dumbbell rack (left side)

- White gym towel (on bench)
- Black water bottle (on bench)
- Wall mirror (background)
- Lat pulldown machine (background)
- Seated row machine (background)

## Scene 3: THE CTA (10 seconds)

### Creative Intent
Direct-to-camera close. He's one of them — a gym bro recommending something real to another gym bro. The blue NeoCell tub is prominently visible. He speaks with genuine conviction, not salesman energy. The pointing-down gesture at the end directs attention to where the product tag/link lives. The final frame should linger — it's the frame a viewer sees as they decide to tap the link.

### Environment
Same gym, but the framing is tighter so less environment is visible. The flat bench with the blue NeoCell tub is directly to his left at hip height, label clearly facing camera. The chrome dumbbell rack is visible in the background but softer/slightly out of focus due to the tighter framing creating a natural depth-of-field effect. The gym floor, a sliver of the wall mirror, and the edge of a weight machine are just barely visible — enough to maintain the gym context without cluttering the frame. The warm overhead lighting continues, but in this tighter shot it creates a subtle rim-light effect on the edges of his shoulders and traps.

### Camera
Medium-close shot, framed from mid-stomach to just above his head. Camera is at eye level, about 2.5 feet away — slightly closer than Scene 1. Natural handheld feel but more stable than the previous scenes (the energy is calmer now — he's delivering, not moving). At the 7-second mark when he does the point-down gesture, the camera tilts down very slightly to follow his hand, then comes back up to his face for the final nod.

### Lighting
Same warm-white overhead LEDs. In this tighter framing, the light hits differently: his face is more evenly lit with softer shadows under his jaw and cheekbones. The overhead angle creates a subtle highlight along the top of his shoulders and the crown of his buzz-cut head. The blue NeoCell tub catches the warm overhead light, making the blue color and white text pop. The background is slightly darker due to fall-off, creating natural separation between him (subject) and the gym environment (context).

### Actor Action
He faces camera directly, squarely. His posture is relaxed-confident: shoulders back but not tense, chest open, one foot slightly in front of the other. He holds the dark shaker bottle casually in his right hand at his side. His left hand is free and he uses it for natural talking gestures.

**Seconds 0-3:** He speaks directly to camera, maintaining steady eye contact. His expression is earnest and genuine — he's recommending something he actually uses. He gestures with his left hand as he talks (open palm, natural conversational movement). When he says "NeoCell," he briefly glances at the tub to his left and his left hand extends toward it in a presenting gesture.

**Seconds 3-7:** He brings his attention back to camera. "If you train hard, you need this" is delivered with a single definitive nod, eyes locked on camera, left hand making a subtle fist (emphasis, not aggression). His expression is pure conviction.

**Seconds 7-10:** "Link's right there" — he breaks the intensity with a casual smile, and his right hand (still holding the shaker) or his left hand drops to point downward toward the bottom of the frame where the product tag would be. The gesture is quick and casual, not dramatic. He finishes with a slight upward nod (the universal bro-acknowledgment), holds eye contact for a beat, and a small genuine smile. The last 2 seconds are him just standing there, confident and still, with the product visible beside him — the lingering frame.

### Props in Frame
- Blue NeoCell Collagen Bio-Peptides tub (on bench to his left, label facing camera — PROMINENT)
- Dark translucent shaker bottle (in his right hand, casual hold)
- Flat weight bench (partially visible, supporting the tub)
- Chrome dumbbell rack (background, slightly out of focus)
- Edge of wall mirror (background)
- Black rubber gym flooring
