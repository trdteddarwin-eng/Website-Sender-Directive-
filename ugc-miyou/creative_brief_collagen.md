# Creative Brief: NeoCell Collagen Bio-Peptides UGC Ad

**Product:** NeoCell Collagen Bio-Peptides Powder (blue tub, grassfed, unflavored, 20g/scoop)
**Avatar:** Muscular young Black man (shirtless, athletic build)
**Format:** TikTok Shop UGC | 9:16 vertical | 25 seconds
**Budget:** $5 max on KIE (Kling 3.0)
**Date:** 2026-02-24

---

## 1. Format Choice: Problem-Solution + Flex

**Why this format wins for collagen + gym audience:**

The avatar's physique is the ad. A shirtless muscular guy stops the scroll on fitness TikTok -- that's biological. But the twist is: instead of the typical "here's my workout" content, we subvert expectations. The hook is the physique, but the message is "the thing you're NOT doing is holding you back." That creates a knowledge gap the viewer has to close.

Pure testimonial is too passive for this avatar. Day-in-my-life wastes the physique reveal. Problem-Solution lets us use the body as proof, then introduce collagen as the missing piece most gym bros overlook.

**Emotional arc:** Curiosity (scroll stop) --> Recognition (I lift but I'm missing something) --> Discovery (collagen is the answer) --> Action (tap the link)

---

## 2. Story Arc

| Beat | Emotion | What Happens |
|------|---------|--------------|
| **HOOK** (0-2s) | Scroll-stop curiosity | Avatar faces camera, confident energy, delivers a provocative line that challenges the viewer |

## 4. Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1: THE HOOK (5 seconds)

**What happens:** Avatar is in a modern gym, standing near a dumbbell rack, shirtless, looking directly at camera. He has a confident, almost confrontational expression -- like he's about to tell you something you need to hear. Slight forward lean toward camera, one hand gesturing. The energy is "listen up."

**Environment:** Modern gym interior. Black rubber flooring, chrome dumbbell rack behind him, a flat bench to the side. Overhead LED strip lighting with warm-white tones. Slightly dim ambient -- feels like an evening gym session. A water bottle and towel on the bench sell the "mid-workout" feel.

**Camera:** Medium shot, chest-up framing. Slight handheld wobble for UGC authenticity. Camera is at eye level, about 3 feet away -- like a friend holding their phone.

**Voiceover:** "Bro, if you're in the gym every day and you're NOT taking collagen... you're leaving gains on the table."

**Pacing:** He starts talking immediately on frame 1. No dead air. The provocative statement carries the full 5 seconds. His expression shifts from confident to slightly incredulous ("you're really not doing this?") by the end.

**Transition to Scene 2:** He turns away from camera to grab a dumbbell off the rack. Last frame = him reaching for the weight, side profile.

---

### Scene 2: THE WORKOUT + PRODUCT REVEAL (10 seconds)

**What happens:** Two-part scene. First half (5s): avatar is doing heavy dumbbell curls, controlled reps, focused expression, sweat visible. He's working. Second half (5s): he sets down the dumbbell, walks to a gym bench where the blue NeoCell tub and a shaker bottle are sitting. He picks up the tub, scoops powder into the shaker, shakes it. Casual, practiced -- this is routine for him.

**Environment:** Same gym, wider shot now showing more of the space. Weight machines visible in the background, a wall mirror reflecting the scene. The blue NeoCell tub is visible on the bench from the start of the scene (product placement before the explicit reveal). Gym towel draped over the bench. Shaker bottle is a dark/clear bottle.

**Camera:** Starts medium-wide to capture the curl motion. As he transitions to the product, camera follows him (slight pan) and tightens to medium shot as he scoops. The blue tub should be clearly visible and readable.

**Voiceover:** "I'm talking joints that don't crack when you squat. Recovery that actually keeps up. Hair, skin, nails -- all of it. Twenty grams of grass-fed collagen per scoop. No taste. Just throw it in your shake."

**Pacing:** The workout footage is FAST energy -- powerful reps, muscles working. When he transitions to the product, the pace softens slightly -- it's the "reveal" moment. The scoop-into-shaker action should feel effortless and routine, not performative.

**Transition to Scene 3:** He takes a drink from the shaker, satisfied expression. Last frame = him holding the shaker, slight nod, about to turn back to camera.

---

### Scene 3: THE CTA (10 seconds)

**What happens:** Avatar faces camera again, shaker bottle in one hand, NeoCell tub visible beside him on the bench. He's speaking directly to camera with confident, genuine energy. Not yelling, not whispering -- just a real recommendation from one gym bro to another. He gestures toward camera (the "you" gesture), then does a casual point-down (indicating the product link). Ends with a nod and slight smile.

**Environment:** Same gym, back to the medium/close framing. The blue NeoCell tub is in frame, label facing camera. Background is slightly out of focus (shallow depth of field feel) to keep attention on him and the product. The gym setting remains visible enough to maintain context.

**Camera:** Medium-close, chest and face visible. Stable but with slight natural handheld movement. As he does the point-down gesture for CTA, camera might tilt slightly to follow his hand (drawing attention to where the product link would be).

**Voiceover:** "This is what I use every single day. NeoCell. Grass-fed bio-peptides. If you train hard, you need this. Link's right there."

**Pacing:** Confident and deliberate. Each sentence lands. "NeoCell" gets a beat of emphasis. "If you train hard, you need this" is the money line -- delivered with direct eye contact energy. "Link's right there" is casual, almost throwaway -- like he knows you're already convinced.

**End frame:** Him holding the product with a slight nod. This is the frame that lingers as viewers decide to tap the link.

## 6. Scene Continuity Plan

**Scene 1 --> Scene 2:** Last frame of Scene 1 is him turning toward the dumbbell rack, reaching for a weight. Scene 2 picks up with him already curling. The turn creates a natural visual bridge.

**Scene 2 --> Scene 3:** Last frame of Scene 2 is him drinking from the shaker, nodding. Scene 3 picks up with him lowering the shaker and addressing camera. Same position, same setting -- seamless.

**Consistency rules:**
- Same gym environment across all 3 scenes (lighting, flooring, equipment style)
- Avatar stays shirtless throughout (physique is part of the sell)
- The blue NeoCell tub appears in Scenes 2 and 3 (planted in background of Scene 2, prominent in Scene 3)
- Shaker bottle appears in Scenes 2 and 3
- Lighting stays consistent: overhead LED, warm-white, slightly dim evening gym

## 8. Audio Direction

**Voiceover:**
- Voice: Young male, confident, casual (ElevenLabs "Liam" or similar)
- Tone: Talking to a friend at the gym, not presenting to an audience
- Speed: Slightly faster than conversational but clear
- Emphasis words: "NOT," "gains," "twenty grams," "grass-fed," "every single day," "need this"

**Music:**
- Lo-fi gym beat, deep bass, minimal melody
- Volume: 20-25% under voiceover
- Purpose: Energy without distraction

**SFX (subtle):**
- Dumbbell clank when he picks up weights (Scene 2)
- Shaker bottle rattle when he shakes it (Scene 2)
- Optional: subtle whoosh on text overlay transitions

---

## 9. Kling 3.0 Prompt Direction (for Scene Designer)

Each scene prompt must paint the FULL frame. Kling does not create environments from nothing -- the prompt must describe everything visible: the gym, the lighting, the equipment, the person, the action, the mood.

**Key prompt elements to include:**
- "muscular young Black man, shirtless, athletic build" (avatar reference)
- Full gym environment description every time
- Specific action with body mechanics (not just "standing in gym")
- "raw authentic phone video UGC feel" in every prompt
- Camera framing instruction
- Lighting description

**What to avoid:**
- Vague prompts ("man in gym with product")
- Assuming Kling will infer the setting
- Prompts that only describe the person without the environment
- Overly cinematic language (we want UGC, not a Nike commercial)

## 10. Success Metrics

This ad wins if:
- **0-2s:** The physique + provocative line stops the scroll (hook retention >70%)
- **5-15s:** The workout footage + benefit stack keeps them watching (mid-video retention >50%)
- **15-25s:** The CTA converts watchers to clickers (CTR >2%)
- **Overall:** Feels like a real gym bro's recommendation, not a scripted ad
