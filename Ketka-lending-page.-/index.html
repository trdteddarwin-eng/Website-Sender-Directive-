Common Questions

Everything You Need To Know

What is AI Automation?

AI Automation handles the simple, repetitive tasks that eat up your day — follow-up emails, lead scoring, data entry, scheduling, and more. These run automatically in the background, 24/7, so nothing falls through the cracks.

What types of businesses is this for?

We specialize in serving local service businesses in North Jersey, including law firms, dental practices, and HVAC companies.

How much does it cost?

We offer two tiers: a Basic AI Receptionist at $500/month, and a Premium tier with proactive appointment reminders and advanced features at $1,000/month plus a one-time setup fee.

Can the AI schedule appointments?

Yes. Sophie integrates with Google Calendar to book, reschedule, and confirm appointments in real time during the call.

Do you provide a CRM?

Yes. Every plan includes access to our built-in CRM where you can view all your leads, call history, and caller details in one place. You'll have full visibility into every interaction Sophie handles.

Can Sophie integrate with my existing CRM?

Absolutely. We can connect Sophie to most popular CRMs including Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, Clio (for law firms), and others. Lead information and call summaries can be automatically pushed to your system so your workflow stays seamless.

What happens after each call?

You receive detailed call summaries and lead information. Our premium tier includes post-call analytics and business intelligence reporting.

Does it really sound like a human?

Sophie is built on advanced voice AI technology and trained specifically for your industry. Callers often can't tell they're speaking with an AI.

How quickly can I get started?

Most businesses are live within a few days. We handle all the setup, including customizing Sophie's knowledge base and integrating with your calendar, CRM, and workflows.

What if the AI can't answer a question?

Sophie is trained to gracefully handle edge cases — she'll capture the caller's details and ensure you can follow up personally.

What are Agentic Workflows?

Agentic Workflows handle complex, multi-step jobs from start to finish. Multiple AI agents work together — researching, building, reviewing, and delivering — so you get complete results without lifting a finger. Think of it as hiring a team of specialists who never sleep.

What's the difference between AI Automation and Agentic Workflows?

AI Automation handles simple, repetitive tasks — things like sending follow-up emails, updating your CRM, or scoring leads. Agentic Workflows tackle bigger, multi-step jobs where AI agents work together to research, create, review, and deliver complete results. Think of automation as your tireless assistant, and agentic workflows as your full project team.

How long does the pipeline take per lead?

The full pipeline — research, site generation, quality review, deployment, and email — typically completes in under 2 minutes per lead. You can process hundreds of leads per day with zero manual work.

What industries does the automation work for?

The pipeline is designed for service businesses — HVAC, plumbing, dental, legal, auto repair, and more. If your business has a website and serves local customers, the pipeline can research, build, and reach out on your behalf.

Can I use both AI Automation and Agentic Workflows?

Absolutely. Many clients pair AI Automation (to handle everyday tasks like follow-ups and data entry) with Agentic Workflows (for complex projects like outreach campaigns and content creation). Together, they cover both the simple and the sophisticated.
